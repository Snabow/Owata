### Eligibility contract

- Input: `{ role, requiredCapabilities }` against a validated `ProviderRegistry`
- Output: `{ status: "ELIGIBLE", bindings }` ordered by `priority ASC`, then `binding_id ASC`
- Or: `{ status: "NO_ELIGIBLE_BINDING", bindings: [] }` (not an exception)
- Malformed config: fail closed (`RouterError`)
- Duplicate capabilities in a profile: rejected
- Unknown fields: rejected
